MoneyPrinter Creator Assistant

A private desktop creator tool used by one independent content creator to manage original videos for the About Everything channel.

Purpose

  • Upload creator-owned videos as private for review
  • Add creator-written titles, descriptions and tags
  • Upload translated subtitle tracks and localized metadata
  • Read channel analytics for content improvement

The application is not publicly accessible, does not serve third-party users, does not automate engagement, and does not sell or share YouTube data.
